Gemini AI · Privacy

When Gemini is enabled, your questions and relevant recent chat messages are sent to Google. For family-specific questions, selected public family information (names, relationships, ages, birthday month/day, and Birthday Fund figures when asked) is included. For unrelated general questions, Ask Pono does not attach the family database. Ask Pono can calculate exact date differences locally from full birthdates stored in this app and pass verified results to Gemini without automatically transmitting those birthdates. Firebase login details and private user IDs are not included; anything you type is sent. Google may use free-tier submissions to improve products. Avoid sensitive personal details, especially about children.

Default answers: With your consent, Gemini answers first whenever online. If Google's quota is reached or Gemini is unavailable, local answers take over automatically. Ask Pono has no app-imposed daily question limit. AI never edits family records. Gemini has no live web search in this free configuration; news and current-event answers must not be treated as verified live reporting.
